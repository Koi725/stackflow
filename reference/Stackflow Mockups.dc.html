1Stackflow — screens & system

One board. Four columns. Nothing else.

Assumptions. Modernist tokens (Archivo, ink/red, 0 radius, 2px rules) flipped to a dark ground for the product; the light ground stays for docs/marketing. "Cinematic" comes from scale, motion and the single red — no gradients or glass fills. Priority is colour on the card's top rule (red / salmon / grey); labels are three treatments of the same palette (solid red, solid white, outline). Members see fewer controls: no Manage, no filters, no delete, locked teammate cards.

Motion system. Login → board: left-to-right clip wipe (700 ms, ease .7/0/.2/1). Card drag: a lifted ghost (rotate 1.5°, scale 1.04, red outline), target column tints red; on drop the ghost morphs into its resting place with a FLIP transform and siblings slide to close the gap (420 ms). Modals: scrim fade + surface rise (350 ms). Every frame is live in the prototype.
